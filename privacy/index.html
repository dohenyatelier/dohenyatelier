Privacy Policy

Last updated September 28, 2026

Overview

This policy explains what information Doheny Atelier (“we”, “us”) collects through dohenyatelier.com and through our Pinterest integration, and how it is used. In short: this is a portfolio website, it has no accounts, forms or analytics, and we do not sell or share personal information.

Information we collect

We do not ask you for personal information on this website. If you email us at hey@dohenyatelier.com, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message, and we use it only to reply to you.

Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or analytics cookies. The site saves a few small preferences in your own browser’s storage—your light or dark theme, your accessibility settings, and whether you have already seen the intro animation—so they carry across pages. This information never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Third-party services

Like any website, loading a page shares standard technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) with the services that deliver it. This site is hosted by GitHub Pages, uses fonts from Adobe Fonts, loads a scrolling library from unpkg, and shows some videos and thumbnails from YouTube and Vimeo. Each of these services handles that information under its own privacy policy.

Pinterest integration

We use the Pinterest API to publish our own portfolio work to the Doheny Atelier Pinterest account: when we add a case study to this website, a board is created for it and its project images are posted as pins linking back to the case study. The integration is used only by Doheny Atelier, on our own account. It does not collect, store or process any information about Pinterest users or visitors to this site, and the Pinterest access credentials are kept private and used solely to post our own content.

Children

This website is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. Questions about privacy can be sent to hey@dohenyatelier.com.
