Nautica Oceans

Designed to celebrate an active, beach-inspired lifestyle, the sustainable Nautica Oceans fragrance relaunch features a custom-tooled bottle and eco-conscious secondary packaging that perfectly captures the contemporary, water-based, and alcohol-free essence of the original scent.

Client Nautica
Industry Fragrances & Personal Care
Services Packaging Design
Product Design

About the Project

Since debuting its first men's fragrance in 1992, Nautica Fragrances has developed a portfolio of more than 40 products. These aspirational scents resonate with a diverse consumer demographic — those who are inspired by the beach, vacation, and a comfortable, sun-drenched active lifestyle.

Kate, the founder of Doheny Atelier, designed the primary and secondary packaging of Nautica Oceans, as well as its ancillaries including a body mist and mini. Nautica sought to bring back a fragrance from the past with a contemporary edge, while making it distinguishable from the original and enhancing its sustainable story. She built a custom-tooled primary and cap that captures the ocean's vibrant blue, cool ocean-side adventurer aesthetic, and water-based, alcohol-free hero claims. The secondary pack encapsulates a lifestyle that looks just as good as the fragrance smells.

01 Nautica Oceans Fragrance (Primary & Secondary)

A deep blue bottle crafted from a custom mold features a deeply embossed emblematic sail on the front, paired with a matching waterscape secondary package. This design portrays the sustainable formula within through a contemporary lens, mirroring the ocean with its rectangular silhouette and an embossed sail on the cap.

02 Nautica Oceans Fragrance, Travel Sized (Primary & Secondary)

An ideal companion for setting sail, the compact Nautica Oceans travel-sized fragrance offers a portable scent that ensures easy mobility, whether you are packing for a new adventure or boarding a speedboat abroad.

03 Nautica Oceans Body Mist

Perfect for active individuals in need of a midday refresh, this body mist covers all your bases. Featuring a stunning wrap-around waterscape that complements the hero product's secondary packaging, it smells exceptional and looks completely the part.
